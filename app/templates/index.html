What is a GAN?

Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) use two neural networks—a generator and a discriminator—competing in a game-like process to produce realistic data. The generator creates synthetic examples, while the discriminator evaluates them against real data. Over time, both networks improve, resulting in highly convincing outputs.


1. Generator: Learns patterns from real data and tries to create convincing “fakes.”

2. Discriminator: Judges how authentic or fake the generated samples are.

3. Adversarial Training: Both networks compete, pushing each other to improve.

4. Realistic Outputs: GANs can generate images, molecules, or text nearly indistinguishable from real data.

5. Broad Applications: From art and media to drug discovery, GANs fuel innovation in diverse fields.

6. Ongoing Research: Challenges like stability and ethical considerations remain active areas of study.
